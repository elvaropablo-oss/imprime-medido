# ImprimeMedido

## Product truth

ImprimeMedido helps people turn millimetres into a physical print they can verify. The product is useful when labels, cutting guides or small templates must survive browser and printer scaling without requiring design software.

## Primary journeys

1. Print and measure a 100 mm calibration square.
2. Calculate independent horizontal and vertical correction percentages.
3. Lay out custom labels on A4 or Letter paper.
4. Generate a rectangle with a requested physical size.
5. Print the result at 100% and verify it with a physical ruler.

## Constraints

- Calculations and label text remain in the browser.
- Millimetres and print scale must always be explicit.
- Print-only sheets must retain exact CSS physical dimensions.
- The interface must distinguish scaling errors from margin or feed offsets.
- The app should work on mobile for preparation, while clearly supporting desktop printing.
